> what_this_is

A working session, not a tutorial. The mess is the point.

Most "building in public" is a highlight reel. This isn't. We show up once a week, screen shared, and build the real thing — including the parts where we're wrong.

01

Live and unpolished

Uncertainty, design debates, dead ends, the occasional broken build we fix on air. No script, no retakes.

02

The twist is the product

What we're building is the system that publishes this show — an AI teammate that drafts each episode's write-up. You're watching it learn.

03

Ship weekly, publish before perfect

Every session ends with something shipped and written up. Impostor syndrome optional but likely.
